Do I need a pendrive?
No. Upload directly from your phone or PC — no pendrive needed.

Enter your OTP at the kiosk, or use Direct Print or QR Print to collect your print.
What files can I print?
PDF, Word, and image files — PDF works best.
Any setup required?
Zero. Open a browser, upload, and confirm your job.
