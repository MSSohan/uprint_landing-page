We are grateful for the opportunity to introduce UPrint, our campus printing solution, to your esteemed institution. UPrint is a cloud-based printing platform designed to make student printing queue-free, cashless, secure, and convenient, while working with your existing printing infrastructure.

How Uprint Works

Students can submit documents directly from their mobile phones or computers and collect their printed documents from a dedicated UPrint kiosk with a simple QR code scan. The solution can benefit both the university and its students by reducing printing time, operational workload, and infrastructure-related costs.

How UPrint Benefits EDU Students

Print Anytime, Anywhere

Students can upload documents directly from their phones or pc and pick up their prints from a nearby UPrint-enabled printer-reducing printing time by 50% or more.

Simple & Flexible Payment

EDU can provide students with printing credits or charge on a per-page basis, helping offset printing and maintenance costs.

Complete Printing Visibility

EDU gets a centralized view of its printer network, including who is printing, how many pages are printed, and where printing occurs.

Secure File Handling

Uploaded documents are automatically deleted immediately after printing, minimizing the risk of sensitive student documents being stored or exposed.

Better Student Privacy

Students don't need to log in to shared or unfamiliar computers to print. They can print directly from their own devices, providing a more private and secure printing experience.

A Better Printing Experience for Everyone

Faster for students. More secure for users. Easier for EDU to manage.
