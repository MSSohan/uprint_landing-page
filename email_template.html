Introducing Uprint - Smart Campus Printing. Uprint is a cloud-based printing platform designed to make student printing queue-free, cashless, and convenient - while working with your existing printing infrastructure.

How Uprint Works

Students can submit documents from their mobile phone or computer and collect their prints through a dedicated Uprint kiosk.

1

OTP Print

Send a document remotely and securely collect the print using a one-time password (OTP).
2

QR Direct Print

Scan the kiosk's QR code with a mobile device and send the document directly for printing.
